This webinar is ideal for individuals diagnosed with Type 2 diabetes, prediabetes, insulin resistance, metabolic syndrome, or anyone experiencing high fasting blood sugar who wants to explore natural, Ayurvedic approaches to metabolic health and reversal.

The webinar covers the common root causes of high blood sugar, the traditional Ayurvedic understanding of Madhumeha (diabetes), lifestyle and dietary factors that affect metabolism, and natural ways to optimize insulin sensitivity through Ayurveda.

Yes. During the live Q&A session, participants will have the opportunity to ask their specific questions directly to Acharya Manish Ji and his team of experienced Ayurvedic doctors.

Yes. The webinar is designed to help participants understand metabolic health from an Ayurvedic perspective. It provides insights into natural lifestyle, dietary, and wellness practices that can safely support blood sugar control alongside your current medical care.

You will gain a much better understanding of the underlying causes of insulin resistance, learn practical lifestyle and dietary adjustments, discover Ayurvedic principles that support long-term metabolic health, and receive expert guidance to help you make informed decisions on your diabetes reversal journey.
