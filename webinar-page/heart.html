Frequently Asked Questions

What will I learn about cardiovascular care in this live webinar?

You will learn the Ayurvedic perspective on managing high blood pressure, clearing heart blockages, and lowering cholesterol naturally. Our experts will also highlight daily habits that harm your heart and offer practical lifestyle and dietary shifts you can start the very next day.

Who should attend this webinar?

This webinar is highly recommended for anyone dealing with high blood pressure, cholesterol, blockages, or breathing issues, as well as anyone looking for natural ways to strengthen their heart.

Can I ask questions about my specific heart condition during the webinar?

Yes. During the live Q&A session, participants will have the opportunity to ask their questions directly to Acharya Manish Ji and the team of experienced Ayurvedic doctors.

Is there any fee to attend the webinar?

Yes. A nominal registration fee may be charged to confirm your participation and reserve your seat. The fee details will be shared during the registration process.

Will I get a diet plan during the webinar?

During the session, our experts will share general guidelines for a heart-healthy, pH-balanced diet. However, because every individual's body type is different, a fully customized diet plan is provided during a personal consultation based on your specific reports.

Can I attend this webinar if I haven't been diagnosed with a heart condition yet?

Yes. Prevention is a major part of Ayurveda. The webinar covers the early warning signs of heart issues and provides daily habits and dietary tips to keep a healthy heart strong and prevent future problems.
