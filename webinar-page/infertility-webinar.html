Frequently Asked Questions

Who should attend this infertility webinar?

This webinar is ideal for couples trying to conceive, individuals diagnosed with PCOS, low sperm count, hormonal imbalance, irregular periods, or anyone looking to understand natural Ayurvedic approaches to fertility and reproductive health.

What topics will be covered during the webinar?

The webinar covers the common causes of male and female infertility, the Ayurvedic understanding of reproductive health, lifestyle and dietary factors affecting fertility, and natural ways to support conception through Ayurveda.

Will I be able to ask my personal fertility-related questions?

Yes. During the live Q&A session, participants will have the opportunity to ask their questions directly to Acharya Manish Ji and the team of experienced Ayurvedic doctors.

Is this webinar helpful if I have already tried other fertility treatments?

Yes. The webinar is designed to help participants understand fertility from an Ayurvedic perspective and provides insights into natural lifestyle, dietary, and wellness practices that may support reproductive health alongside appropriate medical care.

Is there any fee to attend the webinar?

Yes. A nominal registration fee may be charged to confirm your participation and reserve your seat. The fee details will be shared during the registration process.

What benefits can I expect after attending the webinar?

You will gain a better understanding of the possible causes of infertility, learn practical lifestyle and dietary recommendations, discover Ayurvedic principles that support reproductive health, and receive expert guidance from Acharya Manish Ji and his team to help you make informed decisions about your fertility journey.
